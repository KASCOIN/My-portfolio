2024 vs 2025 Ozone Comparison

Project Overview

This meteorological project compares ozone distribution between 2024 and 2025 across Nigeria, highlighting spatial differences in atmospheric composition over the two annual periods. The map classifies areas as significant decrease, slight decrease, stable or minimal change, slight increase, and significant increase to show where ozone conditions changed between the two years.

  • 2024–2025 Ozone Comparison: Compared ozone levels between the two years to identify spatial variations and shifts in atmospheric distribution across the country.
  • Geospatial Visualization: Produced a clear and professional map layout with a legend, boundary context, and classified ranges for easy interpretation.
  • Atmospheric Trend Interpretation: Identified regions where ozone concentrations either declined or intensified from 2024 to 2025, providing insight into atmospheric variability.
  • Environmental Insight: Used the comparison to assess how changes in ozone distribution may relate to air quality, atmospheric conditions, and regional environmental monitoring.
  • Scientific Communication: Converted atmospheric data into a presentation-ready map suitable for research, analysis, and environmental decision support.

Technical Stack: Google Earth Engine, QGIS, GIS Cartography, Meteorological Data Analysis, Spatial Classification, Ozone Mapping

Applications: Atmospheric monitoring, environmental assessment, air quality analysis, and climate-related regional planning.
